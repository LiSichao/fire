# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md) · [Project README](README.md)

## Our commitment

The FIRE project aims to be a respectful, constructive, and welcoming space for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or point of view. Participants should focus on improving the project and discussing ideas on their merits.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, kind, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not the worth or identity of contributors.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and curiosity.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information or real financial data.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and make room for differing levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory or demeaning language, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibilities and enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Responses may range from a warning to temporary or permanent exclusion, depending on severity and context.

For a private concern, contact a maintainer through the contact method available on their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's reporting tools. Do not post sensitive reports as public issues.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, code reviews, and other spaces where someone is representing the FIRE project. It also applies when project-related interaction materially affects the safety or well-being of community members.

## Reporting and interpretation

Reports will be reviewed as promptly as reasonably possible. Maintainers will handle reports discreetly to the extent practical, while taking appropriate action. This document may be revised as the project and its community evolve.
